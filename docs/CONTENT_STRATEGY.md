# Rovil Coffee & Tea - Content Strategy & Buyer Psychology Guide

## 1. The Core Commercial Objective
The target audience consists of **international commodity importers, commercial roasters, distributors, and private label procurement directors**.
They are executing **multi-ton containerized purchase orders ($100k+)**.

The website avoids retail fluff (*"a warm cup of happiness"*, *"shop now"*, *"waitlist"*).
Instead, it leads with **trade authority, origin authenticity, export licensing, and technical grading**.

---

## 2. Key B2B Value Propositions
1. **Licensed Exporter in Kenya:** Regulated under Kenya's Coffee Directorate with complete export documentation compliance.
2. **True Farm Provenance:** Cultivated in high-altitude volcanic soils of Central Kenya (Mount Kenya / Aberdares ranges), ensuring high density and distinct Kenyan acidity.
3. **Commercial Volume Supply:** Capacity for 20ft Full Container Loads (~320 x 60kg bags / 19.2 MT) and 40ft FCL bulk shipments.
4. **Lab-Grade Quality Assurance:** SCA cupping standards, certified moisture levels (<12%), defect count analysis, and GrainPro hermetic bag protection.
5. **Multimodal Global Logistics:** Direct overland transit via Nairobi ICD / SGR rail freight to Kilindini Harbour, Port of Mombasa, connecting directly to global shipping lanes (Rotterdam, Hamburg, Felixstowe, New York, Yokohama).
